# Multi-Selection

Grouping or moving a handful of apps one at a time gets tedious. Multi-select
lets you gather several tiles first, then act on all of them together.

## Entering selection mode

Use the select button in the search bar area to switch into multi-select
mode (the same control toggles you back out). Once it's active, tapping a
tile toggles whether it's part of the current selection instead of
launching it.

## Dragging a selection

Drag any tile that's part of a selection of two or more, and Launchy moves
the whole set together rather than just the one tile you grabbed. Drop the
group onto a folder, or onto another app, to add every selected app into
that folder in one motion instead of repeating the drag per app.

You can also turn a selection directly into a brand-new folder without a
drop at all: with two or more apps selected, the same "create folder" action
available while multi-selecting groups them immediately, and can walk
straight into naming the new folder.

## Clearing a selection

Toggle multi-select mode off again (the same button, or `Escape`) to drop
the current selection and return to normal single-tile behavior.
