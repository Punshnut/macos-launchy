# Gestures

If you'd rather keep your hands off the keyboard entirely, Launchy's paging
works with trackpad and mouse gestures too.

## Paging

- **Swipe left or right** anywhere on the grid to move between pages, the
  same motion you'd use to flip between Launchpad pages on macOS.
- **Scroll wheel up or down** does the same thing, and works on both
  classic mouse wheels and Magic Mouse.
- **Smooth scrolling accumulates.** A single small flick doesn't have to
  cross the whole threshold at once, several little scrolls in the same
  direction add up and still flip the page once they cross it. You don't
  need one deliberate big swipe every time.

This matters most in fullscreen mode with a lot of pages, browsing by
gesture is often faster than reaching for arrow keys if your hand's already
on a trackpad or mouse.

## Dismissing

Tap or click anywhere on the background (not a tile or folder) to close
Launchy quickly, no need to find a keyboard shortcut if your hand's already
off the keyboard.

See also: [Keyboard Shortcuts](Keyboard-Shortcuts) for the equivalent
keyboard-driven paging.
