# Tips and Tricks

The small moves that make Launchy feel fast once they're in your fingers.
Each one is explained in more detail on the linked page.

## Getting around

- **Jump straight to a page.** Hold `Control` and tap `1`-`9` (or `0` for
  page 10). See [Keyboard Shortcuts](Keyboard-Shortcuts).
- **Press `Escape` repeatedly.** It cancels a rename, leaves multi-select,
  closes a folder, clears the search, and finally hides Launchy, one layer
  per press.
- **Search by what you remember.** Search matches English and your system
  language, ignores accents and capitalization, and finds fragments of names
  and bundle IDs. See [Search](Search).
- **Rename apps to how you think of them.** The custom name is what search
  matches, so "Visual Studio Code" can become "code". See
  [Organizing Your Apps](Organizing-Your-Apps).

## Arranging

- **Hold `Option` while dragging** one app onto another to make a folder.
- **Hold `Shift` while dragging** to swap two tiles without reshuffling
  everything around them.
- **Drag to the edge and wait** to carry an app across pages in one motion.
- **Right-click the background** to create a new folder, or an app to
  **Move to Page** or **Create New Page** exactly where you want it.
- **Use multi-select** to move or group several apps at once. See
  [Multi-Selection](Multi-Selection).

## Closing and returning

- **Click the background** to close Launchy. Inside a folder it closes the
  folder first.
- **`Shift`- or `Option`-click the background** inside a folder to close
  Launchy entirely; reopen within 90 seconds and the folder is still open.
- **Reopen within 90 seconds** and you're back on the same page. Change the
  longer-term behavior under Settings → Visuals. See
  [Settings Overview](Settings-Overview).

## Making it yours

- **Switch to vertical paging** if your gestures feel more natural up and
  down. See [Gestures](Gestures).
- **Hide the Dock and menu bar icons** and rely on the hotkey
  (`Cmd` + `Shift` + `Space` by default) for a completely out-of-the-way
  launcher.
- **Back up before a big reorganization.** Settings → Shortcuts has manual
  export and restore, and Launchy keeps automatic backups too.
