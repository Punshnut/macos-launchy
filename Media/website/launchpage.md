# Launchy — the open-source Launchpad alternative for macOS

Launchy brings a fast, Launchpad-like fullscreen experience plus a compact Floaty HUD for quick launches. It scans your apps automatically, keeps icons cached, and stays ready on every Space without getting in the way.

## What you get

- Fullscreen grid that mirrors Launchpad with richer controls and custom backgrounds.
- Floaty Mode HUD for quick app launches and toggles without leaving your current context.
- Instant search with bilingual matching, diacritic folding, and keyboard-first controls.
- Hot corners and global shortcuts to show/hide Launchy or jump between fullscreen and Floaty.
- Right-click tools for renaming, hiding, moving apps, creating folders, and revealing in Finder.
- Auto-updating, notarized builds, MIT licensed, and free forever.

## Highlights to showcase

- **Responsive grid**: 7x5 pages with cached icons and optional ~/Applications scanning.
- **Context-aware controls**: Menus for folders, Dock/menu bar items, and status updates.
- **Visual flexibility**: Blur, transparent, or solid backgrounds with a color palette.
- **Presence settings**: Launch at login, toggle Dock and menu bar icons independently.
- **Keyboard flow**: Arrow paging, `Return` to launch, `Cmd` + `,` for settings, and `Control` + number for direct page jumps.

## Get started in seconds

1. Download the latest build from <a href="https://github.com/Punshnut/macos-launchy/releases/latest">GitHub Releases</a>.
2. Move Launchy to `/Applications` and open it.
3. Set your show/hide shortcut (defaults to `Cmd` + `Shift` + `Space`) and, if you like, a fullscreen ⇄ Floaty toggle.
4. Optional: enable Launch at Login so Launchy is ready on every reboot.
