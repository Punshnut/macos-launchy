# Fullscreen and Floaty Mode

Launchy gives you the same grid of apps in two shapes, so you can pick
whichever one fits what you're doing right now.

## Fullscreen mode

This is the classic Launchpad experience: an edge-to-edge canvas that takes
over the screen, with a blurred or solid background behind it. It's the
mode to reach for when you want to actually look at and organize your apps,
not just launch one and get back to work.

Fullscreen is where the heavier organizing tools live: right-click any tile
to rename it, hide it, move it, create a folder, or reveal it in Finder.
Pages hold up to 7x5 apps each, and you can jump straight to any page by
holding `Control` and tapping a number key.

Use fullscreen when you're:

- Setting up Launchy for the first time and arranging your grid.
- Cleaning up clutter, building folders, or hiding apps you rarely use.
- Just browsing, the way you would with the old Launchpad.

## Floaty mode

Floaty mode is a compact HUD that overlays whatever you're doing instead of
taking over the screen. It's built for speed: summon it, type a couple of
letters, hit `Return`, and you're back in whatever you were doing, with the
app you wanted now open.

Floaty mode is best when you're:

- Mid-task and just need to jump to one more app without losing context.
- Using Launchy the way you'd use a quick-launch palette rather than a
  full-screen browser.
- Working on a smaller screen where a fullscreen takeover feels heavy.

## Switching between them

Both modes share one grid, so nothing about your layout, folders, or custom
names changes when you switch. Set a fullscreen <-> floaty toggle shortcut
in Settings (Shortcuts tab) and flip between them instantly, use whichever
one matches the moment instead of committing to one or the other.

See [Keyboard Shortcuts](Keyboard-Shortcuts) for the toggle shortcut, and
[Organizing Your Apps](Organizing-Your-Apps) for what you can do with the
grid once you're in fullscreen.
